Léo Pichon

Associate Professor
Institut Agro Montpellier · UMR ITAP, DéMO team

I work on crowdsourcing of spatial observations in agriculture (farmsourcing): how to design such projects, assess the quality of the observations collected, develop methods that compensate for their imperfections, and use them for decision support at the regional scale. My main field of study is the monitoring of vine water status with the Apex-Vigne project (~2,000 winegrowers and advisors contributing each year).

My teaching covers spatial information and digital tools for decision support in agriculture. I head the AgroTIC specialisation (MSc, 2nd year).

Teaching

At Institut Agro Montpellier (SABP department), I teach about tools and methods for spatial information and their use for decision support in agriculture, from 3rd-year bachelor to 2nd-year master level (about 250 teaching hours per year). I favour a competency-based approach, project-based learning and close links with the professional world.

  • AgroTIC specialisation, MSc 2nd year, head since 2023 (in partnership with Bordeaux Sciences Agro)
  • Course Environmental Data Processing and Analysis, MSc 1st year, coordinator since 2022
  • Course Spatial information in agriculture and the environment, BSc 3rd year, coordinator since 2022

I designed a card game for learning geomatics concepts without GIS software, now used in several institutions, and in 2024 I launched a network for sharing teaching practices among French universities of agronomy and veterinary.
